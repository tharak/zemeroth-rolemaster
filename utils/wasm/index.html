      How to play & rules status

      Initiative is rolled each round. The highlighted fighter may move up to half their movement (25 feet for these profiles), then take one action. Tap an empty hex, review the blue route, and confirm with Move here. Choose Attack or Cast, select a target, then confirm. Use Pick target to target allies. Blur targets the caster. Other fighters remain inspectable.

      Every action uses Normal timing and ends the turn. End turn waits without attacking. Parry reserves the equipped melee weapon’s OB against all incoming melee attacks; each defense spends only what is needed to prevent hits and criticals. Missiles and spells bypass it. Casting is always unprepared.

      Target and destination menus support keyboard input. Playback can be paused or stepped. Replay is read-only. This experimental adaptation retains the documented rule limitations. Source & rule coverage · Original game and artwork by ozkriff and contributors.
