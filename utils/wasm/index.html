    How to play & rules status

    Pick a fighter, choose a phase and action, then declare. You can assign one action in each phase and up to 100% activity. Tap an empty hex to choose a movement destination. Choose a friendly target in the Target menu for healing or blessings. Blur always targets the caster.

    All four party members share your commitment. Undeclared fighters wait. Enemies plan independently before your choices. The engine resolves Snap, Normal, then Deliberate actions in initiative order.

    This is an experimental Rolemaster adaptation. The current rule coverage and remaining source-audit work are listed in the repository. It is not yet the complete planned ruleset.
